Q3 FY2026 Earnings Excerpt — Northwind Research Labs

Revenue grew 12% year-over-year to $4.2 billion. Operating margin expanded to 18.5%, up 90 basis points from the prior year. Gross margin was 61.2%.

The board approved a $500 million share repurchase program. Cash and cash equivalents totaled $1.1 billion at quarter end. Capital expenditures were $85 million.

No material litigation was disclosed in the period. Guidance for Q4 revenue is $4.3 billion to $4.5 billion. Headcount increased by 120 employees to support the cloud platform.
